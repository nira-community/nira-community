Awesome Low Level

We created this wiki as a guide and collection of resources mainly to help complete beginners and self-learners take their first step into low-level programming. Getting started can be confusing when you don't know what to learn, where to start, or what topics are actually important. So we made this to give a clear path to follow, starting from electronics and fundamentals and slowly going towards the more specialized fields they are interested in, without making things more confusing.
